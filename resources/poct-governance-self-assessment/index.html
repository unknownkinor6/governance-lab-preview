Purpose and audience

Who it is for

PoCT leads, clinical governance and quality leads, and registered managers who want a structured view of their point-of-care testing arrangements.

It can help prepare for an internal review, plan improvement work or decide where specialist support would help.

What is included

Proposed contents

  • Guidance on completing the self-assessment
  • Assessment sections across the main areas of PoCT governance
  • Space to record evidence references and notes
  • A priorities and action summary
