Why review PoCT governance

Point-of-care testing brings results closer to patients. The arrangements supporting it can span clinical teams, devices, locations and reporting systems.

A governance review helps organisations consider how responsibilities, operator authorisation, documentation and quality assurance fit together - and where further evidence or action may be needed.

What the self-assessment offers

The PoCT Governance Self-Assessment is designed to support a structured discussion about organisational oversight.

It helps teams consider:

  • Where responsibilities and accountability are clear.
  • Which arrangements are supported by accessible evidence.
  • Where assurance depends on informal knowledge or assumptions.
  • What needs further investigation, review or improvement.

Its value lies in the evidence and discussion behind the responses, rather than the score alone.

Who it is for

The resource is intended for PoCT leads, clinical governance and quality leads, directors of nursing, registered managers and other healthcare leaders with responsibility for PoCT oversight.

It can be completed collaboratively, involving relevant clinical teams and pathology or other specialist colleagues where appropriate.

What it covers

Ownership and accountability

Responsibility for PoCT oversight, decisions and follow-up.

Governance structures and escalation

How concerns, exceptions and risks are raised, reviewed and acted upon.

Clinical pathway integration

How testing and results fit into clinical workflows and decision-making.

Training and authorisation

How the organisation oversees operator training, competency and permission to test.

Documentation and traceability

Whether records allow testing processes, responsibilities and decisions to be understood and reviewed.

Assurance, audit and learning

How the organisation checks whether its arrangements remain effective as services change.

How the tool can be used

The self-assessment can support internal governance discussions, help identify evidence requiring review and inform improvement priorities.

It can also help teams decide whether a focused specialist review or a more detailed PoCT governance gap analysis would be useful.

Scope and limitations

This is an organisational governance resource. It does not assess analytical performance, establish operator competence or replace device verification, pathology oversight or a detailed review of practice.

Responses reflect the information entered by the organisation. They are not independently verified, and a completed assessment does not certify compliance or guarantee an inspection outcome.

When further support may help

Where the assessment identifies uncertainty, The Governance Lab can discuss a separately scoped review. This may include examination of supporting evidence, prioritised findings and recommendations to support management and board-level oversight.

Further consultancy is optional and agreed separately.
