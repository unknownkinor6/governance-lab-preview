Biography

Susana Rodrigues is a Registered Nurse and founder of The Governance Lab, bringing experience in clinical leadership, clinical governance, quality improvement and patient safety.

Her work spans clinical audit, policy development, risk management, staff training and competency assessment. She helps healthcare organisations understand gaps in their systems and turn findings into practical improvement plans, working alongside existing leaders and teams.

Susana also offers specialist expertise in point-of-care testing (PoCT), including quality assurance, operator competency, traceability and governance oversight. Her approach combines frontline clinical understanding with a clear focus on accountability and safer care.
