Sample PoCT improvement action plan

How findings become actions, proposed responsibilities and completion evidence

Day 0 is the date management receives and reviews the findings. All actions are Proposed. Owners and targets require agreement; they are illustrative planning targets, not regulatory deadlines. Prompt clinical assessment takes precedence where a safety concern is identified.

Proposed actions, owners, targets and completion evidence
RefActionProposed ownerTargetCompletion evidence
F01aReview the four operator record gaps; determine interim controls.Clinical managerDay 0Recorded decisions and staff communication.
F01bReconcile all 12 operators and resolve competency and authorisation gaps.PoCT leadDay 10Register, assessments and authorisation decisions.
F01cEstablish monitoring of operator status and review dates.PoCT leadDay 15Approved process, owner and schedule.
F02aInvestigate the two unsuccessful checks, establish whether patient testing occurred before resolution and assess further action.Clinical managerStart Day 0; initial review Day 2Investigation, specialist advice as needed and recorded decisions.
F02bClarify escalation and conditions for resuming testing; brief active operators.PoCT leadDay 10Approved procedure and briefing or assessment records.
F03aDocument responsibilities, deputy arrangements and escalation.Clinical managerDay 15Approved responsibilities and communication.
F03bIntroduce an assurance report and review it at governance meeting.Governance leadBy Day 30Report, meeting record and assigned actions.

Review points

Day 2Review unresolved safety concerns.
Day 15Check operator records and revised processes.
Day 30Review the first assurance report and overdue actions.
Day 45Assess whether subsequent records demonstrate effective implementation.
