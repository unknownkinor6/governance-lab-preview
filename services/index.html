01Core service

Clinical governance review

An independent review of agreed governance arrangements, including accountability, oversight, documentation and assurance.

Who it is for

Directors of nursing, registered managers, and clinical governance and quality leads in independent hospitals, clinics and care providers.

The problem it addresses

You need an independent view of how well your governance arrangements work, and whether the evidence supports what is written down.

Possible scope

  • Accountability and reporting lines
  • Oversight and assurance processes
  • Documentation and evidence
  • One service, department or concern

Possible outputs

  • A findings report
  • Prioritised risks
  • A practical improvement action plan

02

Clinical audit and improvement support

Support with a defined audit, review of existing findings or development of an improvement plan.

Who it is for

Quality and governance leads, audit leads and service managers with a defined audit need, or findings that need to become action.

The problem it addresses

Policies are in place, but you need to understand whether practice and evidence support them, or audit findings need a coordinated improvement plan.

Possible scope

  • Designing or carrying out a defined audit
  • Reviewing existing audit findings
  • Developing an improvement plan

Possible outputs

  • An audit tool
  • A findings summary and recommendations
  • A follow-up review
Enquire about audit support
03

Policy and procedure development

Review or development of agreed clinical policies and procedures, with attention to how they will work in practice.

Who it is for

Organisations updating clinical policies, introducing a new service or procedure, or responding to audit findings.

The problem it addresses

Documents need to be current, clear and workable for the people who use them, not only in place.

Possible scope

  • Review of agreed policies and procedures
  • Development of new documents
  • How the documents will work in practice

Possible outputs

  • Revised documents
  • A summary of changes
  • Implementation recommendations
Enquire about policy work
04

Training and competency frameworks

Support with defining training requirements, assessment processes, authorisation and review arrangements.

Who it is for

Clinical leads, practice educators and managers responsible for training, competency assessment and authorisation.

The problem it addresses

You need to show who is trained, who is currently competent and authorised, and when each review is due.

Possible scope

  • Training requirements
  • Competency assessment processes
  • Authorisation and review arrangements

Possible outputs

  • A competency framework
  • Assessment documents
  • A training matrix
Enquire about competency work
05

Risk and incident learning support

Review of agreed clinical processes or recurring themes to identify system weaknesses and improvement opportunities.

Who it is for

Governance and patient safety leads looking at recurring themes, or at a process that keeps raising concerns.

The problem it addresses

Audit findings or recurring incidents need a coordinated improvement plan that looks at the system, not only individual events.

Possible scope

  • Review of an agreed clinical process
  • Thematic review of recurring issues
  • System weaknesses and improvement opportunities

Possible outputs

  • A thematic review
  • Recommendations
  • An action plan

Work is offered only within my professional expertise and an agreed scope.

Enquire about risk and learning support
