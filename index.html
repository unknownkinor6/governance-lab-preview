Where do you need support?

You may have an established governance team but need an independent perspective, specialist knowledge or additional capacity for a particular project.

  • Are policies supported by consistent practice and clear evidence?
  • Do audit findings and recurring incidents lead to completed improvements?
  • Can you demonstrate effective oversight of your PoCT service?

I work alongside your team to understand the concern, review the evidence and agree on practical next steps.

How I can help

Clinical governance reviews

Review accountability, reporting, documentation and assurance arrangements to identify gaps and priorities.

Audit and improvement support

Develop focused audits, review findings and create improvement plans with clear responsibilities and measures of progress.

Policies and competency frameworks

Develop or review policies, procedures and training and competency arrangements that support everyday clinical practice.

Specialist PoCT consultancy

Review responsibilities, operator competency, quality assurance, traceability and governance oversight.

Specialist support for point-of-care testing

Using a device is only one part of a reliable PoCT service. Clear ownership, competent operators, quality assurance and traceable results also need effective oversight.

Support can include a focused review of a specific concern, a wider governance gap analysis or help implementing agreed improvements.

A focused PoCT review

An agreed review of a defined area of your service, with written findings and prioritised recommendations. Scope and fee are confirmed before work starts.

Clear findings and practical next steps

Depending on the agreed project, you receive:

  • A written account of the evidence reviewed and gaps identified.
  • Prioritised recommendations.
  • An action plan with proposed responsibilities and timescales.
  • Follow-up support where agreed.
